Thong bao hien hanh

Version: 1.2 Effective Date: 01/01/2024

1. Muc dich thu thap

He thong thu thap du lieu de quan ly tai san, bao mat mang va ho tro ky thuat.

2. Du lieu thu thap

Cau hinh may (CPU, RAM, Disk), trang thai online/offline, ten user dang nhap, dia chi IP.

3. Cam ket KHONG thu thap

Noi dung lien lac (email, chat), lich su duyet web, phim go (keylogging), anh man hinh (screenshot).

4. Thoi han luu tru & Truy cap

Luu tru trong 24 thang. Chi quan tri vien he thong duoc quyen truy cap.

5. Quyen cua nguoi dung

Yeu cau kiem tra du lieu ca nhan, khieu nai neu co sai pham.
